Meetings

Your request is emailed to the Rotaract Club of Rubaga through FormSubmit, a form-forwarding service. After you press Send you may be asked to tick an "I'm not a robot" box. Only the club name, meeting time and venue are shown publicly.

What happens next

  1. We check the details with your club.
  2. We add the club and its meetings to Fellowship Finder.
  3. Your club appears on Fellowship Finder, usually within a week.
