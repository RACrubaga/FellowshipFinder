Finding a fellowship

For everyone

Open Fellowship Finder and it shows Rotaract and Rotary clubs meeting today within 5 km of you, soonest first. Each club shows its next meeting date, time and venue, with directions one tap away.

Sharing your location

  1. When the page opens, your browser asks to use your location. Tap Allow.
  2. Clubs are now measured from where you are. The line under Your location says how accurate it is.
  3. Prefer not to share? Choose an area such as Rubaga or Ntinda from the list next to Use my location.

If you tapped Block earlier, a yellow notice explains how to turn location back on. In short:

  • Android (Chrome): tap the icon to the left of the web address, then Permissions or Site settings, and set Location to Allow. Make sure your phone's location (GPS) is switched on.
  • iPhone (Safari): tap aA in the address bar, then Website Settings, and set Location to Allow. Also check Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services is on and Safari is set to While Using.

Then tap Try again on the notice.

Distance, club type and day

  • Distance: 2, 5, 10 or 15 km from you. The page starts at 5 km.
  • Club type: All, Rotaract or Rotary.
  • When: starts on Today. Open the list to pick Tomorrow, any day in the coming week, Any day in the next 7 days, or Any time.
  • Soonest first / Nearest first: sort the list by meeting time or by distance.
  • Map / Radar: the map shows clubs as pins on real streets; the radar shows them by distance and direction from you. Tap a pin or dot to jump to that club.

Reading a club card

The ticket on the left shows the day, date and start time of the club's next meeting. Next to it:

  • TodayWhen the meeting is: Today, Tomorrow, This Friday, or In 9 days. Happening now means it has started.
  • In personHow the club meets: in person, in person + online, or online.
  • CancelledThat meeting won't happen; the card shows the reason and the next date.
  • Changed for this meetingThe time or venue is different this once.

Buttons on each card:

  • Directions opens Google Maps with the venue as your destination.
  • WhatsApp appears only if the club agreed to share its number.
  • Copy details copies the club name, next meeting, venue and a directions link, ready to paste into a chat.

When nothing shows

If no club meets at the time and distance you chose, the page tells you, then offers only options that actually have clubs, for example 2 clubs within 10 km today or 3 clubs within 5 km in the next 7 days. Tap one to switch to it. If there is nothing within 15 km, it names the nearest club with its next meeting and directions.

Add it to your phone

  • Android (Chrome): tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen or Install app.
  • iPhone (Safari): tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.

It then opens full-screen from its own icon, like an app.

Is your club missing?

Fellowship Finder is run by the Rotaract Club of Rubaga, whose team adds and updates club information. If your club isn't listed, or its details are out of date:

  • Fill in the short Add your club form with the meeting day, time and venue, or
  • email info@rotaractrubaga.org.

The team checks the details and lets you know when your club is live, usually within a week.

Light or dark? The page opens in light mode. Use the button in the top-right corner to switch; your choice is remembered on that device.

For club admins

People given access by the Rotaract Club of Rubaga

Fellowship Finder is maintained by the Rotaract Club of Rubaga team. Club admins they invite can keep their own club's details, meeting times and venue up to date.

Getting access

  1. Ask the Rotaract Club of Rubaga team for access at info@rotaractrubaga.org. Accounts are by invitation only.
  2. Open the invitation email and tap the link. It opens the Fellowship Finder login page, already signed in.
  3. Open My account (bottom of the club list), enter your name, and set a password of at least 10 characters.
  4. Next time, tap Login at the top of the public page and sign in with your email and password.

Forgot your password? On the login page enter your email and tap Forgot password?, or Email me a sign-in link to get in without one. Links work once and expire within an hour.

Registering your club

  1. Tap Register a club.
  2. Enter the club name, type and RI club number (from My Rotary), plus area, website, WhatsApp number and club email.
  3. Tick the consent box only if the club agrees to show its WhatsApp number and email publicly. Otherwise they stay private.
  4. Tap Save club. You become its admin straight away.

Pending until approved. New clubs are hidden from the public page until the Rotaract Club of Rubaga team approves them. You can add meetings in the meantime.

Adding a meeting

  1. Open your club, go to the Meetings tab and tap Add a meeting.
  2. Give it a name, such as Fellowship or Board meeting.
  3. Choose How often:
    • Every week, then the day.
    • Every other week, then the day and the date of one upcoming meeting, so the right weeks are used.
    • Certain weeks of the month, then the day and which weeks, for example 1st and 3rd, or Last.
  4. Set the start time, length and format (in person, in person + online, or online only). Check the preview line: it lists the next dates.
  5. Enter the venue name and place the map pin. Either tap the map where the venue is (drag the pin to adjust), tap Use my current location if you are at the venue, or paste a Google Maps link.
  6. Add a directions note if it helps (gate, landmark, floor), then tap Save meeting.

Getting a Google Maps link: open the venue in Google Maps, tap Share, copy the link, and paste it into the box under the map.

Cancelling or moving a date

  1. Open your club and go to Cancel or move. It lists the next 10 dates.
  2. To cancel: tap Cancel next to the date, type a short reason people will see, and confirm.
  3. To move: tap Move, then set the new date, time and venue if different (you can paste a new map pin), add a reason, and save.
  4. Made a mistake? Tap Undo next to the date.

Changes show on the public page straight away.

Keeping details current

Every month or so, open Details and tap Confirm details and meetings are current. This tells the Rotaract Club of Rubaga team your details are still right. Only admins see it.

Privacy

  • Your location never leaves your phone. Distances are worked out on your device and are not sent or stored.
  • A club's WhatsApp number and email are shown only if the club ticked the consent box.
  • Admin accounts hold only an email address and name, used to sign in.
  • The Add your club form is emailed to the Rotaract Club of Rubaga. Your own contact details are used only to reply to you.

Questions

Why can't I see my club on the public page?

  • It hasn't been added yet. Use the Add your club form.
  • It is still Pending approval, or marked as not active.
  • Its meeting is set to hidden (untick and re-tick Show this meeting on the public page).
  • It doesn't meet on the day chosen under When, or it is beyond the distance chosen. Try Any time and 15 km.

What time zone are meetings in?

Kampala time (East Africa Time).

A club's details look wrong. What should I do?

Contact the club before you go, and let the Rotaract Club of Rubaga know through the Add your club form (choose Update my club's details) or at info@rotaractrubaga.org.
