Finding a fellowship

For everyone

Open Fellowship Finder and it shows Rotaract and Rotary clubs meeting today within 5 km of you, soonest first. Each club shows its next meeting date, time and venue, with directions one tap away.

Sharing your location

  1. When the page opens, your browser asks to use your location. Tap Allow.
  2. Clubs are now measured from where you are. The line under Your location says how accurate it is.
  3. Prefer not to share? Choose an area such as Rubaga or Ntinda from the list next to Use my location.

If you tapped Block earlier, a yellow notice explains how to turn location back on. In short:

  • Android (Chrome): tap the icon to the left of the web address, then Permissions or Site settings, and set Location to Allow. Make sure your phone's location (GPS) is switched on.
  • iPhone (Safari): tap aA in the address bar, then Website Settings, and set Location to Allow. Also check Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services is on and Safari is set to While Using.

Then tap Try again on the notice.

Distance, club type and day

  • Distance: 2, 5, 10 or 15 km from you. The page starts at 5 km.
  • Club type: All, Rotaract or Rotary.
  • District: All, D9214 or D9213.
  • When: starts on Today. Open the list to pick Tomorrow, any day in the coming week, Any day in the next 7 days, or Any time.
  • Soonest first / Nearest first: sort the list by meeting time or by distance.
  • Map / Radar: the map shows clubs as pins on real streets; the radar shows them by distance and direction from you. Tap a pin or dot to jump to that club.

Reading a club card

The ticket on the left shows the day, date and start time of the club's next meeting. Next to it:

  • TodayWhen the meeting is: Today, Tomorrow, This Friday, or In 9 days. Happening now means it has started.
  • In personHow the club meets: in person, in person + online, or online.
  • CancelledThat meeting won't happen; the card shows the reason and the next date.
  • Changed for this meetingThe time or venue is different this once.

Buttons on each card:

  • Directions opens Google Maps with the venue as your destination.
  • WhatsApp appears only if the club agreed to share its number.
  • Copy details copies the club name, next meeting, venue and a directions link, ready to paste into a chat.

When nothing shows

If no club meets at the time and distance you chose, the page tells you, then offers only options that actually have clubs, for example 2 clubs within 10 km today or 3 clubs within 5 km in the next 7 days. Tap one to switch to it. If there is nothing within 15 km, it names the nearest club with its next meeting and directions.

Add it to your phone

  • Android (Chrome): tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen or Install app.
  • iPhone (Safari): tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.

It then opens full-screen from its own icon, like an app.

Light or dark? The page opens in light mode. Use the button in the top-right corner to switch; your choice is remembered on that device.

Is your club missing?

Rotaract and Rotary clubs

Fellowship Finder is maintained by the Rotaract Club of Rubaga. If your club isn't listed, or its meeting details have changed, send them to us and we'll add or update it, usually within a week.

Option 1: use the form

  1. Open the Add your club page.
  2. Choose Add my club or Update my club's details.
  3. Fill in the club's name, district, type, when it meets, start time and venue. A Google Maps link to the venue helps us place it exactly.
  4. Tap Send request. You may be asked to tick an "I'm not a robot" box.

Option 2: send an email

Write to info@rotaractrubaga.org and include:

  • the club's full name, whether it's a Rotaract or Rotary club, and its district (D9214 or D9213)
  • the RI club number, if you have it
  • when the club meets (for example Every Tuesday or 1st and 3rd Friday) and the start time
  • the venue name and area, ideally with a Google Maps link
  • anything else useful, such as online meetings or a public contact for the club

Getting a Google Maps link: open the venue in Google Maps, tap Share, and copy the link.

Privacy

  • Your location never leaves your phone. Distances are worked out on your device and are not sent or stored.
  • A club's WhatsApp number and email are shown only if the club ticked the consent box.
  • The Add your club form is emailed to the Rotaract Club of Rubaga and asks only for club details.

Questions

Why can't I see my club on the public page?

  • It hasn't been added yet. Use the Add your club form or email info@rotaractrubaga.org.
  • It doesn't meet on the day chosen under When, or it is beyond the distance chosen. Try Any time and 15 km.

What time zone are meetings in?

Kampala time (East Africa Time).

A club's details look wrong. What should I do?

Contact the club before you go, and let the Rotaract Club of Rubaga know through the Add your club form (choose Update my club's details) or at info@rotaractrubaga.org.
